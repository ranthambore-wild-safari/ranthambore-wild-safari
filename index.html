Ranthambore is split into 10 safari zones, allotted by the forest department. Zones 1–5 are closed on Wednesdays and Zones 6–10 on Tuesdays. Best season: October to June.

What You’ll See

TIGERS, FORT & FOREST

Ranthambore is known for tigers that are often seen in daylight, set among lakes, dry forest and ruins.

Bengal Tiger

The reason people come. Sightings depend on the animals’ own movement, so no zone can promise one.

Ranthambore Fort

A 10th-century hill fort inside the reserve, part of the UNESCO World Heritage Hill Forts of Rajasthan.

Other Wildlife

Leopard, sloth bear, sambar, spotted deer, crocodiles and many birds across lakes and grassland.

Good to Know

FAQ

Which is better, Gypsy or Canter?

Gypsy is a 6-seater, better for families and photographers. Canter is a 20-seater and costs less per person.

Can you guarantee a tiger sighting?

No. Tigers are wild animals and sightings depend on the day and the zone allotted.

What is the best time to visit?

October to June is the safari season. Core zones are closed during the monsoon.

What do I need to carry?

A valid photo ID for every guest (passport for foreign nationals), light earthy-coloured clothes, water and a hat.
